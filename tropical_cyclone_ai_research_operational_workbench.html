Empirical Rules vs. Autonomous Deep Feature Learning

Classic Dvorak Technique (VDT)

  • Cloud Feature Mapping: Evaluates curved banding arcs, central dense overcast (CDO), shear, and eye pattern geometry.
  • Index Scale: Assigns $T$-number ($T1.0$ to $T8.0$ at $0.5$ steps), converted to Current Intensity ($CI$).
  • Operational Sensitivity: High subjectivity and inter-analyst variance ($\pm 5\text{ kts}$ error rate in ~50% cases).
  • Failure Modes: High cirrus canopies obscuring low-level circulation centers (LLCC); data-sparse basins like the North Indian Ocean.

Advanced Dvorak Technique (ADT)

  • Automated Computation: Automated center identification; calculates brightness temperature ($T_b$) differentials between eye and eyewall.
  • Smoothing Constraints: Imposes a strict 6-hour moving-average filter to eliminate unphysical high-frequency fluctuations.
  • Operational Bottleneck: The 6-hour moving average introduces a 6-to-12-hour latency during Rapid Intensification (RI) events.
  • Recon Dependency: Re-calibration required in basins without routine aircraft reconnaissance.

Representation Learning (Pradhan et al. & CNN-TC)

  • Direct Radiometer Ingestion: End-to-end 2D-CNNs extract spatial features directly from geostationary infrared imagery without manual rules.
  • Rotational Invariance: Rotation-blended CNNs (Chen et al., 2019) sample multi-angle IR fields to handle cyclonic rotational symmetry.
  • Performance Benchmark: Achieved intensity estimation RMSE of $4.06\text{ m/s}$ ($7.89\text{ kts}$) and MAE of $3.23\text{ m/s}$.
  • Consensus Parity: Performance matches or exceeds ADT and SATCON platform consensus estimates.

Cascaded Stage Architecture (TCICENet)

  • Decoupled Network Tasks: Separates intensity analysis into TCIC (Classification) and TCIE (Regression) modules.
  • Stage Specialization: Categorizes systems into developmental regimes prior to running fine-grained wind regression.
  • Spatial Optimization: Rigorous evaluation showed $170 \times 170\text{ pixels}$ spatial crop provides optimal vortex envelope context.
  • Validation Scale: Validated over 1,001 cyclones (1981–2019) in NW Pacific ($\text{RMSE} = 8.60\text{ kts}$, $\text{MAE} = 6.67\text{ kts}$).

Decoupled Subsystem Pipeline Architecture

Model Subsystem 01

Vortex Center Detection

YOLOv8 / K-Means DBSCAN Object Backbone

  • Input: Raw INSAT-3D/3DR Thermal IR ($10.8\,\mu\text{m}$) imagery.
  • Operation: Pinpoints spatial low-level circulation center (LLCC).
  • Operational Defense: Remains robust even when vertical wind shear displaces upper convective canopy away from surface center.
Output: Bounding Box Coordinates $[X_c, Y_c, W, H]$
Model Subsystem 02

Cascaded Intensity Classifier

TCICENet / Dvorak-Inspired Vision Transformer

  • Input: Cropped $170 \times 170$ vortex patch + Scatterometer surface winds.
  • Operation: Stage classification (Depression $\rightarrow$ Super Cyclone) followed by wind regression.
  • Bias Reduction: Prevents peak wind underestimation common in naive MSE models.
Output: Intensity Category & MSW (kts)
